Post Summary

Lily Ray shares her experience with Google’s AI Mode, specifically the shopping feature powered by Personal Intelligence.

She explains that AI Mode can use connected data sources like Gmail and other Google apps to personalize shopping recommendations based on a user’s past behavior, including:

Past purchases
Travel history
Long-term user activity (in her case, 10+ years of data)

She highlights that AI Mode provides:

Personalized product recommendations
Multiple price options in a sidebar
The ability to browse websites without leaving AI Mode
A more conversational shopping experience compared to traditional Google Shopping

She also notes that this system can influence user behavior by introducing them to retailers they wouldn’t normally visit.

Why this matters

This post shows the direction Google is moving in: hyper-personalized, AI-driven shopping experiences.

It highlights how search is evolving from keyword-based results to user-history-based recommendation systems, which strengthens Google’s position in agentic AI and e-commerce.



Lily Ray – LinkedIn Post Summary 3

Date: 5 days ago
Source: LinkedIn
**link** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_llm-content-visibility-scanner-algorythmic-activity-7453456039443869696-VNF4?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAFQUTeYBzjWRoBK6Q7uHfnUSEbc6IKppLMw

Post Summary

Lily Ray introduces a custom tool she built called the “LLM Content Visibility Scanner”, designed to analyze how AI crawlers see a webpage.

The tool simulates how non-Google AI systems like:

GPTBot (ChatGPT)
ClaudeBot
PerplexityBot

read a page when JavaScript is not executed (similar to how many LLM crawlers operate).

It helps identify technical SEO and AI visibility issues such as:

Client-side rendering problems
Missing structured data
Paywall-related restrictions
Alt text gaps
Crawlability issues

After scanning a URL, the tool generates a prioritized action plan with specific recommendations for fixes.

She notes that the tool is not a replacement for full technical SEO audits, but rather a quick diagnostic tool for identifying AI/LLM visibility issues.

Why this matters

This post highlights the shift toward AI crawler optimization (LLM SEO readiness).

It shows that modern SEO is no longer only about Google indexing, but also about ensuring content is accessible and readable for AI-based retrieval systems and LLM crawlers.
