Post Summary

Neil Patel explains how AI agents interact with websites differently from humans.

Instead of reading pages visually like users, AI systems focus on structured machine-readable signals such as:

Schema markup
Clear structured content
Working APIs

He emphasizes that if these technical elements are missing, AI agents may fail to properly interpret or include the website in their outputs.

Why this matters

This post highlights a shift from traditional SEO to AI agent optimization, where visibility depends on how machine-readable and structured a website is.

It reinforces the importance of:

structured data (schema)
clean content architecture
API accessibility for AI systems

in order to remain visible in AI-driven search environments.
