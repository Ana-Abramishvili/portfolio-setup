**date** 29/04/2026

**Source:** LinkedIn
**Type:** Market Research / SEO + AI Job Trends

## Summary

This post highlights how the SEO job market is being reshaped by AI-related skills.

Based on data from 946 full-time SEO roles, jobs that mention AI in the title offer significantly higher salaries compared to traditional SEO roles. The median salary for AI-related SEO positions is $113,625, compared to $89,438 for roles without AI requirements, representing a 27% salary gap.

The analysis also shows that many AI-related requirements are not clearly stated in job titles, meaning most listings requiring AI skills are not easily detectable through simple filtering. Additionally, the salary premium increases at mid-level and senior positions, where AI usage becomes an expected skill rather than a bonus.

Kevin notes that the market is shifting expectations: SEO professionals, especially senior ones, are now expected to integrate AI into their workflows to improve efficiency and performance, particularly as teams become smaller or more resource-constrained.

## Key Insight

AI skills are no longer optional in SEO — they are becoming a baseline requirement, and the market is already pricing professionals based on their ability to use AI effectively.

# Kevin Indig - LinkedIn Post 3 (Summary)
**date**29/05/2026
**link**https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_if-you-are-screening-your-next-career-move-activity-7454831817918062592-sYra?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAFQUTeYBzjWRoBK6Q7uHfnUSEbc6IKppLMw
**Source:** LinkedIn
**Type:** Job Market Analysis / AI Skills in SEO Hiring

## Summary

This post highlights a major mismatch in how AI-related SEO roles are identified in the job market.

Only 15.5% of SEO job postings explicitly mention AI in the job title. However, 59.5% of roles require AI skills somewhere in the job description. This means that most AI-relevant positions are not visible through simple title-based filtering.

Jobs that include AI requirements in the description represent a much larger share of the market and still offer a significant salary advantage, with an estimated 25% median salary premium compared to non-AI roles.

The analysis shows that companies are no longer hiring for isolated “AI SEO specialists.” Instead, they expect standard SEO professionals to naturally integrate AI workflows into their work.

Kevin emphasizes that job seekers should evaluate job descriptions rather than titles when assessing opportunities, while employers should recognize that compensation structures are already shifting toward a two-tier system based on AI capability.

## Key Insight

AI skills in SEO are becoming a hidden requirement across most roles. The real market signal is not in job titles, but in job descriptions — where AI competency is already the default expectation.


The SEO industry now rewards system-level thinkers, not just tool users. The highest value comes from combining deep SEO expertise with the ability to design and orchestrate AI-powered workflows at scale.
