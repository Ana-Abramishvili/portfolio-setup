## Summary

This post focuses on why it is becoming easier to win in search by simply putting in more effort than competitors.

Nathan explains that many search results and AI-generated citations are now filled with low-effort, generic AI content. Because of this, even a small increase in quality and effort can create a strong competitive advantage.

He uses the example of the keyword “best SEO books,” where his content has continued to rank over time without updates. The content originally lived on a different domain and was later redirected, but it still performs well in both traditional search results and AI citations.

The main reason for this success is the depth of effort behind the content — he personally read and reviewed each SEO book before writing about them. This level of hands-on experience created differentiation that automated or surface-level content cannot easily replicate.

Nathan emphasizes that most competitors are focused on speed and automation, while very few are willing to invest deeper effort. This creates an opportunity for high-effort content to stand out in both SEO and AI-driven search systems.

## Key Insight

In an environment saturated with AI-generated content, effort and real-world experience become key differentiators. High-quality, deeply researched content can outperform automated output even without frequent updates.

## Summary

This post argues that most discussions around “AI SEO” are overcomplicated, and the core principles of visibility remain largely the same.

Nathan explains that the best way to appear in AI-generated answers is still to focus on strong SEO fundamentals combined with “search everywhere optimization.” This means building visibility not only on your website, but across multiple platforms that AI systems use as retrieval sources.

He emphasizes that SEO remains the foundation (“lead domino”) for visibility in AI systems, but it must be extended beyond traditional websites to include platforms like YouTube and other high-authority content sources.

According to his approach, brands need to ensure they are present across all key retrieval sources that influence AI answers. This includes identifying gaps in visibility and targeting those sources through outreach and content distribution.

He outlines a simple framework for success:

* Strong technical SEO on your website
* Building topical authority
* Expanding authority across owned platforms
* Earning visibility on external trusted sources

## Key Insight

AI visibility is not a separate discipline from SEO — it is an extension of it. Success comes from strengthening SEO fundamentals and expanding presence across all retrieval sources that AI systems rely on.





Winning in modern SEO requires shifting from search-volume-driven keyword research to early trend detection. Identifying and acting on emerging topics before they become mainstream provides a strong competitive advantage.
