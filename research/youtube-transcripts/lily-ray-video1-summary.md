# Lily


## Why this expert
Lily Ray is a leading SEO strategist and VP of SEO Strategy & Research. She focuses on how AI search (GEO/AEO/LLMO) is evolving and how much of it is actually rebranded traditional SEO.

---

## Core Idea
AI search is not a replacement for SEO — it is an extension of existing SEO systems.

Most “new AI SEO concepts” are:
- rebranded SEO fundamentals
- already existing ranking signals
- repackaged optimization techniques

---

## Key Insights

- The SEO industry constantly rebrands “old SEO” into new hype cycles (mobile, voice, AMP, Core Web Vitals, now GEO)
- GEO / AEO / LLMO are largely naming layers, not fundamentally new systems
- Google still dominates search despite AI tool growth

---

- AI search adoption is growing, but not replacing Google
- Most users of ChatGPT still actively use Google search
- AI tools are additive, not substitutive

---

- AI traffic impact is still very small (~1–2%)
- Organic search remains the dominant acquisition channel
- Most businesses still rely heavily on traditional SEO traffic

---

- Many “new GEO tactics” already exist in SEO:
  - chunked content = structured SEO content
  - FAQs / Q&A = existing SEO formats
  - schema markup = structured data SEO
  - multimodal content = YouTube, podcasts, images SEO
  - citations/mentions = digital PR & authority building

---

- AI models depend heavily on search engines (Google/Bing)
- Retrieval systems (RAG) mean SEO is still the foundation of AI visibility
- Without indexed content, AI cannot reliably generate answers

---

- Content visibility in AI systems is often driven by:
  - Wikipedia
  - Reddit
  - YouTube
  - strong SEO authority sites

---

- “Query fanout” in AI search is similar to:
  - long-tail keyword research
  - “People Also Ask”
  - intent clustering in SEO tools

## Strategic Takeaway

AI search does not replace SEO — it depends on SEO infrastructure.

Core conclusion:

> “If SEO is strong, AI visibility follows. GEO is SEO with new packaging.”

Key shift is not tactics, but perception:
- from keyword SEO → to entity + authority SEO
- from clicks → to visibility + brand presence
