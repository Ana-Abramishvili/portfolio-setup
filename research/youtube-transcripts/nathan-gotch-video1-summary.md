## Why this expert

---

## Core Idea
SEO success comes from **daily systems**, not one-time actions.

There are 3 core SEO activity pillars:
1. Optimize existing content
2. Publish new SEO assets
3. Build offsite authority

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Content Optimization (Daily)
- Use Google Search Console to find keywords ranking positions 2–15
- Improve:
  - title tags
  - meta descriptions
  - H1s
  - first paragraph
- Avoid changing URLs for already ranking pages

---

### 2. Topical Coverage Expansion
- Identify missing entities and topics
- Use competitor analysis (Rankability or manual research)
- Improve content depth, not keyword stuffing
- Add sections, paragraphs, or supporting information

---

### 3. Internal Linking Strategy
- Link from related pages to important assets
- If no internal links exist → topical authority is weak
- Internal linking distributes SEO authority across the site

---

### 4. Publishing New SEO Assets
- Create new pages when keyword intent does not match existing pages
- Use “intent splitting” strategy:
  - one keyword → one dedicated page
- Supports both existing pages and expands keyword footprint

---

### 5. AI-Assisted Content Creation (with grounding)
- Do not rely on ChatGPT alone
- Use competitor-driven research:
  - Google
  - ChatGPT
  - Perplexity
  - Grok
- Analyze 40–50 competitors per keyword
- Extract entity frequency and ranking patterns

---

### 6. Knowledge Base System
- Build internal knowledge base (ChatGPT projects / tools / systems)
- Ensure all content is grounded in brand knowledge
- Add consistent brand voice across all outputs

---

### 7. Offsite SEO & Entity Optimization
- Maintain consistent brand information (NAP consistency)
- Optimize brand entity across directories
- Clean and align brand footprint across the web

---

### 8. AI Citations Strategy
- Identify sources AI systems cite (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI)
- Reverse-engineer citation patterns
- Build presence on those platforms
- Brand mentions alone can influence AI visibility

---

## Strategic Takeaway

SEO is not content creation — it is a **daily operating system**:

> Optimize → Publish → Distribute authority → Build entity presence
