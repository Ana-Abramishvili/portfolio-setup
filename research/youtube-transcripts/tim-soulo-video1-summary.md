# Tim Soulo (Ahrefs) — SEO vs AEO Myths Debunked (Video 1)


---

## Core Idea

The idea that “SEO is dying because AI is rising” is mostly driven by bad data interpretation, media hype, and small sample studies.

In reality:
- SEO is NOT collapsing
- AI growth does NOT automatically reduce Google traffic
- The web is expanding (the “pie is growing”)
- Traffic is shifting, not disappearing

---

## Main SEO Myths Debunked

### Myth 1: SEO traffic is down 20–60%

**Reality:**
- Large-scale analysis of ~40,000 websites shows:
  - Overall organic traffic change: ~-2.5% (not -25%)
- This is not a collapse, just a mild fluctuation

**Breakdown by site size:**
- Top 10 sites: traffic increased
- Small sites: traffic increased
- Middle-tier sites: ~-6% to -7% decline

Conclusion: SEO is redistributing, not dying

---

### Myth 2: AI growth is killing SEO

**Reality:**
- AI usage is increasing
- Google usage is not decreasing proportionally
- Both are growing at the same time

Users don’t replace Google with AI — they use both for different purposes

---

### Myth 3: AI Overviews are destroying clicks

**Reality:**
- AI Overviews do reduce click-through rates
- But mostly for informational queries
- Transactional/commercial queries are far less affected

Impact exists, but it is limited and selective

---

### Myth 4: Google is stealing more traffic overall

**Reality:**
- No strong evidence of major traffic theft
- Google organic traffic remains broadly stable
- Changes are uneven, not systemic collapse

---

## Key Data Insights

- Total organic web traffic change: ~-2.5%
- Middle-tier websites are most affected segment
- AI Overviews reduce CTR in informational SERPs (~30%+ in some cases)
- AI referral traffic is still a small portion of total traffic

---

## Important Concept: “The Pie is Growing”

Wrong assumption:
> AI increases → SEO decreases (zero-sum thinking)

Correct reality:
- Web usage is expanding
- AI creates new search behavior, not replacement behavior
- Users split intent:
  - Google → navigation, sourcing, verification
  - AI → summarization, writing, ideation

---

## Why Some Sites Lose Traffic

Traffic drops usually happen due to:
- Informational content being answered directly by AI
- Older or generic content losing relevance
- Lack of topical authority or updates

But:
- New websites and emerging topics often grow
- Niche authority content can still scale strongly

---

## AI vs SEO Is Not a Competition

AI search and SEO overlap but are not the same system:
- SEO = ranking in search results
- AI citations = retrieval + summarization system (RAG)

Overlap exists, but they operate differently

---

## Key Strategic Takeaways

To succeed in both SEO + AI search:

- Focus on topical authority (not random content)
- Build brand mentions across the web
- Keep content updated and fresh
- Avoid blocking AI crawlers (robots.txt mistakes matter)
- Structure content clearly for extraction
- Target emerging topics, not just old “SEO basics”

---

## Final Conclusion

SEO is not dying.

It is:
- evolving
- redistributing
- and coexisting with AI search

The winners are not those who chase hype,
but those who adapt to both systems:
Google + AI retrieval engines together.
