# Aleyda Solis — SEO Tips for 2026 (Video 1) | Summary


---

## Core Idea
SEO in 2026 is evolving into a hybrid discipline where traditional SEO fundamentals still matter, but success increasingly depends on:

- AI systems (LLMs, AI Overviews, agentic web)
- structured data and feeds
- brand authority and entity consistency
- understanding user journeys across multiple platforms

Despite new AI-driven trends, the consensus is clear:
> “Good SEO is still the foundation of visibility in AI search.”

---

## Key Themes from Experts

### 1. Agentic Web & Automation
- SEO is moving toward an “agentic web”
- New protocols (Agentic Commerce, A2A, payment protocols) are shaping automation
- Websites must be prepared for AI agents interacting with content and transactions

---

### 2. Data Feeds Are Becoming Critical
- Search engines and LLMs rely heavily on structured feeds
- Businesses must optimize:
  - product feeds
  - structured datasets
  - APIs and machine-readable content
- Control over data input reduces misinformation in AI outputs

---

### 3. Proprietary Data Advantage
- Unique data = competitive advantage in AI search
- Companies may monetize or restrict access to proprietary datasets in the future
- LLMs depend heavily on external data sources

---

### 4. E-commerce is Changing
- Product packaging may act as “landing pages” (via machine vision)
- Users interact through images, not just text queries

---

### 5. Measurement is Evolving
- Clickstream data becomes essential for understanding AI-driven behavior
- GA4/Adobe Analytics must be used more deeply
- Focus shifts to:
  - prompt behavior
  - multi-touch journeys
  - assisted conversions

---

### 6. Branding + Technical SEO Merge
- Brand management now includes:
  - hallucination control (AI-generated misinformation)
  - brand drift monitoring
  - entity consistency across platforms
- SEO + PR + branding are merging into one discipline

---

### 7. Core SEO Still Matters
Repeated advice across speakers:
- internal linking still matters
- indexation still matters
- schema is becoming even more important
- “just keep doing SEO” remains valid

---

### 8. Schema Becomes More Important Than Ever
- Helps LLMs understand:
  - entities
  - context
  - relationships
- Reduces token cost for AI systems
- Improves machine interpretability

---

### 9. Audience Understanding > Keywords
- SEO shifts toward deep audience analysis:
  - daily routines
  - media consumption habits
  - cross-platform behavior (podcasts, social, video, search)

---

### 10. Brand Alignment is the New SEO Filter
Before publishing anything, ask:
> “Is this aligned with my brand?”

Focus areas:
- content relevance to product/service
- topical authority consistency
- integration with PR and external mentions
- entity optimization across platforms

---

## Strategic Takeaway

SEO in 2026 is not about replacing old SEO with AI SEO.

It is about:

> combining traditional SEO + structured data + brand authority + AI system compatibility

Winning strategies will focus on:
- structured, machine-readable content
- strong brand/entity signals
- audience-first content strategy
- consistent multi-platform presence
- deep analytics integration (not just rankings or clicks)
