## Core Idea
AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini) is fundamentally changing SEO.

Instead of ranking pages like Google, AI systems:
> retrieve → evaluate → generate answers

So visibility is no longer just about rankings, but about being *selected as a trusted source* inside AI-generated responses.

---

## How AI Search Works (RAG Model)

AI search uses **RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)**:

1. **Retrieve** relevant web pages from trusted sources
2. **Generate** an answer using those sources
3. **Cite** selected websites in the final output

👉 If you are not retrieved, you are invisible.

---

## What Determines AI Visibility

AI systems evaluate 4 main signals:

### 1. Relevance
- Content must strongly match the topic
- AI maps brands into “topic neighborhoods”
- Wrong context = ignored

---

### 2. Authority
- Backlinks, brand mentions, reviews, E-E-A-T signals
- AI checks: “Does the internet trust this source?”

---

### 3. Structure
- Clean HTML structure improves extraction
- AI prefers:
  - headings
  - bullet points
  - FAQs
  - clear sections

👉 Disorganized content = skipped

---

### 4. Freshness
- AI strongly prefers recent content
- Especially for fast-changing topics

---

## Key Insight: AI SEO ≠ Traditional SEO

- Ranking #1 on Google ≠ AI visibility
- Example:
  - #1 Google ranking → ~31% AI mention rate
  - Lower rankings → near zero AI visibility

👉 Google SEO and AI citations are different systems.

---

## Biggest Ranking Factor Shift

### Brand Mentions > Backlinks

AI visibility correlates more with:

- brand mentions
- contextual references
- topical association

than traditional SEO signals like backlinks.

---

## Why Brand Mentions Matter

AI learns from patterns across the web:

- repeated brand + topic associations
- contextual relevance across sources

Example:
- Nike → athletic performance
- HubSpot → CRM / marketing

👉 Same mechanism applies to SEO brands.

---

## How to Increase AI Visibility

### 1. Build Brand Mentions
Get mentioned on:
- Reddit discussions
- YouTube videos
- blogs & publishers
- podcasts
- review sites

Goal:
> appear consistently across trusted sources in your niche

---

### 2. Go Deep on One Topic
- Avoid generic content
- Build topic clusters
- Cover all subtopics in depth

👉 AI prefers “topic authority” over general websites

---

### 3. Ensure Crawlers Can Access Your Site
Common issue:
- sites accidentally block AI bots in robots.txt

If blocked:
> AI cannot cite you at all

---

## Content Optimization for AI

AI does not read like humans — it chunks content.

Best practices:
- Put key answers at the top
- Use clear headings
- Use numbered lists
- Use FAQ sections
- Keep sections self-contained

👉 “Lead with answer, then explain”

- Not just third-party sources anymore

Example shift:

## Strategic Takeaway

Winning AI search requires:

- strong topical authority
- structured, easy-to-extract content
- consistent brand mentions across the web
- fresh and updated content
- accessible crawling for AI systems

## Final Principle

> AI search rewards brands that are consistently useful, clearly structured, and widely trusted across the web.

SEO is no longer just about ranking.

- retrievable
