# Koray Tuğberk — Web Entities, Topical Authority & SEO Scaling (Video 2)

## Why this expert
Koray Tuğberk focuses on holistic SEO systems, emphasizing entity-based ranking, topical authority, and data-driven scaling strategies in modern search engines.

---

## Core Idea
SEO is evolving from “website ranking” to “web entity ranking”.

This means Google evaluates:
- entities (brands, authors, systems)
- topical networks
- historical performance signals
not just individual pages.

---

## Key Insights

- Websites should be treated as **web entities**, not static domains
- Historical data (clicks, impressions, traffic growth) builds trust over time
- Trending events can be used to accelerate traffic growth
- Internal linking distributes authority from supporting pages to money pages
- SEO success depends on structured topical maps, not isolated keywords
- AI translation can scale multilingual SEO content efficiently

---

## Important Concepts Mentioned

- Web entity SEO
- Topical authority system
- Historical data influence on rankings
- Cost of retrieval (Google evaluating content value)
- Trending event SEO strategy
- Internal authority flow (link-based distribution)
- Topical map architecture (core / auxiliary / monetization layers)

---

## Strategy Breakdown

Koray describes a layered SEO system:

1. Build traffic through semantically related topics first
2. Collect historical performance signals (clicks, impressions)
3. Use internal linking to transfer authority to money pages
4. Scale using topical maps aligned with business model
5. Leverage trending events for traffic spikes

---

## Strategic Takeaway

Modern SEO is no longer page-level optimization:

> Success depends on building a structured “entity ecosystem” with historical trust signals and topical depth.
