# Koray Tuğberk — Semantic SEO & AI Content Originality (Video 1)

## Why this expert
Koray Tuğberk is a leading voice in semantic SEO and holistic search systems. He focuses on how Google and AI systems evaluate originality, authorship, and content quality beyond surface-level SEO tactics.

---

## Core Idea
Search engines are evolving from keyword-based systems into:
- originality detection systems
- authorship evaluation systems
- structure + intent understanding systems

AI-generated content alone is not enough — human signal and originality are becoming critical ranking factors.

---

## Key Insights

- Google shifts ranking priorities over time (speed → AI detection → originality)
- Originality is evaluated at multiple levels:
  - sentence level
  - paragraph level
  - page level
  - layout and design level
- AI content without human input can trigger quality filters or penalties
- Forums and functional sites (Reddit, ecom, SaaS tools) are more trusted than pure content sites
- Authorship and writing style analysis (stylometry) can identify content ownership
- Search engines may treat ecom vs content sites differently in AI era

---

## Important Concepts Mentioned

- Semantic SEO
- Holistic SEO
- Stylometry (author identification via writing style)
- Content originality detection
- AI content filtering systems
- Function-based websites vs content-only websites
- AI vs human content classification

---

## Strategic Takeaway

SEO is shifting toward:

> “Who created the content + how original it is + what function the site serves”

Not just:
- keywords
- backlinks
- volume

Originality + authorship signals are becoming core ranking factors in AI-driven search.
